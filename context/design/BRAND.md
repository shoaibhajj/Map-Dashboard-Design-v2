# Approved Brand Direction — Connected Operational Intelligence

## Owner-approved wording

«هوية تشغيلية دقيقة، بعمق بصري هادئ وطابع مستقبلي أقرب إلى الخيال العلمي، تحمل روح منظومة IoT متصلة، وتربط الأجهزة والموقع والحركة والزمن والبيانات والتحكم، دون التضحية بوضوح الاستخدام».

Approved in conversation on 2026-10-26. Do not downgrade this back to a tentative concept. Approval covers direction, not every example, token, animation, implementation or performance budget.

Working English expression: precise operational identity, quiet visual depth, science-fiction-leaning futurism, and a connected IoT ecosystem. This is a design direction, not a new product name/tagline.

## Emotional promise

A visitor should want to explore the system. An operator should feel oriented, informed and in control. The same product can be expressive in marketing and calm during incident response.

This platform is a view into a larger IoT project, not merely a GPS map. Express relationships among physical devices/assets, connectivity, sensor readings, location, historical events and operator decisions. Do not imply every imaginable IoT capability is implemented.

## Identity anchors

- Existing default: Petroleum & Mint, dark mode, with independent theme/palette choice preserved.
- Physical-to-digital connection; meaningful layers; precise instrumentation.
- Spatial and temporal continuity; selection has an origin and a destination.
- Futuristic craft: engineered geometry, selective luminous edges, instrument-like detail, purposeful motion and tangible objects.
- Utility remains clear without motion, glow, hover, or 3D.
- Arabic and English are first-class layouts.

## Science-fiction permission, not visual noise

Allowed when useful: restrained HUD-like framing, segmented metrics, signal traces, shallow volumetric regions, luminous route emphasis, layered instrument surfaces, stylized device/vehicle objects, coordinated transitions.

Do not neutralize every futuristic idea merely because operations should be readable. Equally, do not cover roads with bloom, animate every reading continuously, add meaningless hex grids to every surface, or replace recognizable controls with cryptic symbols.

A component may be visually bold while its labels/actions are ordinary. Preserve a quiet background around the most important event. A critical alarm should beat decorative motion in the attention hierarchy.

## Expression levels — working defaults

- E0 utility: fields, dense rows, toolbars. Precise finish, immediate feedback, near-zero decoration.
- E1 signature operations: selected vehicle, trip strip, incident group, cluster. One recognizable relationship or detail.
- E2 discovery: onboarding/feature explanation. Richer narrative and dimensionality.
- E3 focal showcase: one main hero/dedicated demo. Strongest futurism with progressive enhancement.

These are contextual, not approval gates. A cursor or marker edit normally stays E0/E1. No requirement to add 3D everywhere.

## Concept seeds — invent beyond these

- Signal chain: device → reading → event → decision.
- Asset continuity: fleet → selected vehicle → trip/event detail.
- Journey instrument: route + time + stops + source freshness.
- Connected object: compact device/vehicle with meaningful sensor callouts.
- Incident constellation: a spatially anchored group of related events with clear counts/severity.

Do not interpret these as existing product features or mandatory shapes. Explain why the chosen concept fits the current task.

## Tests of originality

Would the design still feel purposeful without its accent color or headline? Is its composition tied to the domain? Is the external inspiration transformed rather than recolored? Could removing an effect improve clarity?

Familiar buttons, dialogs, tables and arrows are allowed. Originality means authored relationships and consistent craft, not novelty for every primitive. Never claim worldwide uniqueness or improved conversion without evidence.
